Our story

Old-school craft, new-school style

Brass & Bristle opened in 2016 in a former tailor's shop on Granite Street. Founders Leo and Marcus wanted a place that felt like the barbershops their grandfathers went to, with a sharper eye for modern styles.

Every cut starts with a proper consultation and ends with a hot towel and a straight-razor neckline. Walk-ins are always welcome, but booking ahead guarantees your chair.
